About Nibarc

Built by people who stay for the build.

Most SAP advice ends at the handover. Ours doesn't — we write the strategy, run the transformation and ship the software that carries it, which means we are still there when the design meets the system.

Why we exist

Enterprises do not struggle with SAP because the software is hard. They struggle because the program loses its own memory: the decision nobody can find, the requirement nobody can trace, the custom object nobody can explain. By go-live the organisation knows less about its own processes than it did at kickoff.

Nibarc was built to close that gap — with consultants who have run the programs, and engineers who build the tooling that keeps the record honest.

The model

We assemble specialists around a decision rather than selling a fixed team. A pricing question pulls a different bench than a cutover question. The engagement is sized to the problem and closes when the problem does.

That only works if the knowledge survives the people. It does, because everything we produce lands in a record you own, and the agents that help produce it run inside your four walls.

What we measure

Outcomes, not deliverables.

A deck is not a result and a milestone is not a result. These are.

Decisions closed

How many open rulings your program is still carrying, and how fast that number falls.

Traceability

What share of requirements, designs and code can be walked back to a named source.

Cost of delay

What each unresolved decision costs per week — stated, not implied.

Our commitment

Your data stays inside your four walls.

Everything we build runs in your tenant, on your models, under your controls. Nothing crosses to another customer and nothing trains anybody else's system. It is the first question every CIO asks us, and the answer has never had an asterisk.

Start somewhere real

Bring us one decision you can't close.

Not an RFP, not a discovery phase. One ruling your program keeps deferring — we'll tell you what it would take to close it, and what it is costing you while it stays open.
