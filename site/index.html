How we work

Small team. Short leash. Evidence throughout.

We are not a body shop and we do not staff by the month. Specialists arrive for the problem in front of them and leave when it is closed.

  1. Name the decision

    We start at the ruling you cannot make yet — not at a discovery phase that defers it.

  2. Assemble for it

    Domain consultants, SAP architects, data people and engineers, chosen for that decision.

  3. Run against a record

    Requirements, rulings and code all trace to a source. Nothing becomes truth because a slide said so.

  4. Leave it running

    Handover, hypercare and the tooling stay with you. We don't rent you a dependency.
