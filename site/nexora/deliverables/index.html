"We don't block anything — sales would never accept it."

"Every order over the limit waits for finance. That is the policy."
S-09 · Finance · 00:12:48 · R-0931

Reading. A control disagreement, not an as-is difference: one room describes practice, the other policy. Six artifacts wait on it: the credit-management design, two test scenarios, the O2C narrative, a RAID entry and a training objective.

Ruling options. (a) Enforce the limit with a release step. (b) Enforce with a warning and a weekly report. (c) Record the policy as not applied and revisit in Realize.

Ruled — option (a). Decider: process owner, Order to cash. Round 3. Rejected: (b), (c).

A ruling round, end to end.

  1. The team returns its own tracker

    The conflict tracker, as a spreadsheet, filled in by the people who own the answers.

  2. Nexora diffs it, cell by cell

    Every changed cell is read against the previous round.

  3. A second agent checks it

    A second model, not the one that read the cell.

  4. Only settled answers are applied

    An answer with no name against it is applied but waits for a decider before it can close.

  5. Effects traced downstream

    Backlog, interfaces, themes, narratives, risks, test seeds, fit evidence.

  6. Every generated deliverable rebuilt, verified, returned

    Undoable: every prior value is kept.
