The Brief

Every word heard. Every decision kept.

The Brief is the process side of Nexora. It reads your discovery workshops, extracts requirements cited to the second, finds the contradictions between rooms, takes each one to the person who owns the answer, and assembles the signed blueprint that everything downstream is derived from.

The Brief, live. The first ruling of the day, with the words behind it and the timestamp.81 rulings waiting · 4,209 requirements captured · 72 fit themes mapped

From a room full of people to a record you can sign.

  1. Listen

    Recordings and documents in, from your own Drive. Originals never leave it.

  2. Extract

    Atomic requirements, each cited to the second. Uncited is invalid.

  3. Contradict

    Disagreements found across sessions, both positions quoted to the second.

  4. Rule

    A named expert decides. The ruling binds, and records what it rejected.

  5. Sign

    A blueprint assembled from rulings. Immutable once signed.

  6. Derive

    Specs, tests, change impact, training and BPMN, all from the signed design.

Aiden

The one who read everything, and remembers who said it.

Aiden is Nexora's reader. Ask it anything about the program — what is waiting on you, where a decision came from, what breaks if you re-rule C-05, what the legacy system actually does today — and it answers from the record, with the ruling, the session and the second attached.

Reads, never writes

Aiden cannot add a fact, correct a ruling or infer a decision. The record changes only through the four human gates.

Every answer cites

Decision, session and evidence chips under every claim. Open the citation before you act on an answer.

Two scopes

Record answers from confirmed memory. As-Is answers only from what Forge read out of the running system — what exists today, nothing to-be.

Cost in the open

Requests and spend are metered per day and shown to the person asking.
