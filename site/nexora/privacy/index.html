Privacy.

What this website collects, and what it does not.

This site is a description of Nexora. It does not require an account, set tracking cookies, or collect personal data through forms.

Analytics

We measure visits with Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused service that uses no cookies and collects no personal data. Page views are aggregated; your IP address is not stored. There is nothing to opt out of because nothing identifies you.

Email

If you write to us at info@nibarc.com, we keep your message to reply to it. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not share your address.

Workshop recordings you bring

Anything you share for a demonstration is processed inside an environment set up for you, is not used to train models, is not mixed with any other customer's material, and is deleted when you ask.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request. No other third-party scripts run on this site.

Changes

If any of the above changes, this page changes first.
